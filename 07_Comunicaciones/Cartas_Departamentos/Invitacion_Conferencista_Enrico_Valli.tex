\vspace{0.1cm}

Dear Prof. Valli,

On behalf of the IN SILICO Research Group and the Department of Biology and Chemistry of the Universidad de Sucre (UNISUCRE), Colombia, it is my pleasure to formally invite you to take part as a \textbf{Keynote Speaker} in the \textbf{International Symposium on Green Science and Circular Economy (SICVEC 2026)}.

Your work on the analytical assessment of olive oil quality and authenticity and on the technological valorization of agri-food by-products speaks directly to two of the symposium's axes, Circular Economy and Green Chemistry and Sustainable Processes. Turning the by-products of an agri-food chain into a source of bioactive compounds is exactly the kind of experience we want to share with the students, researchers and companies of our region. Thank you for confirming your availability.
