## Source for the bio (2026-09-30)

https://posmicrobiologiaagricola.ufv.br/en/faculty/ (Pós Microbiologia Agrícola, UFV), pasted by AC and checked with
Firecrawl. Verbatim:

> Areas of expertise: Yeast prospecting for product and process innovation; Bioprocesses from the perspective of circular
> bioeconomy and sustainability; Development of colorimetric systems for detecting microorganisms.
>
> Associate Professor in the Department of Food Technology and advisor in the Postgraduate Program in Food Science and
> Technology at the Federal University of Viçosa. CNPq DT-2 researcher in the area of Biotechnology and Industrial
> Microbiology. Leader of the research group "Biotechnological Products and Processes in the Food Industry" and
> coordinator of the "Laboratory of Biochemical and Fermentative Processes" and the "Fermentation Laboratory". Member of
> the INCT_Leveduras (CNPq) and Soja + (Fapemig) Research Networks. Creator of the UFV Bioprocess Reference Center, a
> program that aims to foster technological development and innovation in Minas Gerais. Works on research lines for the
> use of microorganisms and their enzymes for the production of fermented products and in the development of detection
> systems for contaminants in food and related industries. Works in partnership with several companies for technological
> development and innovation in biotechnology. [Teaches] Industrial Biochemical Processes (TAL415), Enzymatic Catalysts in
> Bioprocesses (TAL417), Kinetics of Biochemical Processes (TAL416), Biochemical Engineering (TAL615), Immobilized
> Enzymes (TAL617) and Scientific Communication: Research Planning and Writing (TAL770).

## Bio v2 (2026-09-30): Lattes via Escavador

AC sent https://www.escavador.com/sobre/4139832/monique-renon-eller and "mira el pdf en ponentes". Facts added, all
from that PDF (Lattes data as of 2024-07-28; newer work not included):
- Degrees, all UFV: BSc Biochemistry 2004-2008; MSc Agricultural Microbiology 2008-2010 (lytic phages from cheese whey);
  PhD Agricultural Microbiology 2010-2012, obtained 2013 (Pseudomonas phage to control milk proteolysis).
- UFV professor (Magistério Superior, exclusive dedication) 2013-present; also a UFMG link 2022-present (no detail).
- Project 2022-present, "Rede Mineira de Desenvolvimento Tecnológico para incentivo à Economia Circular na cadeia de
  processamento de soja: aproveitamento da casca e melaço" (soy hulls and molasses -> bioethanol and commercial
  products); she is a member (coordinator: Luciano Gomes Fietto). This is the Soja+ network of her UFV profile.
- INCT Leveduras project 2022-present: "conhecer, preservar e utilizar de forma sustentável as leveduras da
  biodiversidade brasileira"; listed as "Coordenador" in her Lattes (probably of the UFV part); bio says "integra".
- Project 2015-2017 (coordinator): yeast selection and bioethanol from cassava; MSc of Maria Ximena Diaz Ramirez (2017,
  "Seleção de leveduras para produção direta de bioetanol a partir de mandioca industrial"), supervised by her.
- By-products: "By-Products as Substrates for Production of Selenium-Enriched Pleurotus ostreatus Mushrooms", Waste and
  Biomass Valorization 2021; coffee vinegar from coffee-bean fermentation water and kombucha SCOBY, undergraduate thesis
  2023; "Produção de biossurfactantes por leveduras utilizando subprodutos industriais", MSc started 2024.
- Supervision, counted from the "Orientou" section: main advisor, completed: 5 PhD, 9 MSc, 50 scientific initiation,
  35 undergraduate theses (85); co-advisor, completed: 13 PhD, 11 MSc. Ongoing not counted.
- Articles: 32 entries with volume and pages in the publications section (our count) -> "una treintena". Journals
  named: Food & Function 2021, Waste and Biomass Valorization 2021, Archives of Virology 2012, Coffee Science 2020,
  Industrial Biotechnology 2022.
- Awards: 1st place, Programa Avança Café (Embrapa Café + TecnoPARQ + LavrasTec), 2023; best oral paper, theme
  "Biotecnologia na Produção de Alimentos, Bebidas e Cosméticos", XXII Simpósio Nacional de Bioprocessos (ABEQ), 2019;
  1st place, Desafio de Pesquisa, Inovação e Negócios, TecnoParq/UFV, 2018. Left out: English test 2023, TCC Conexão
  2021, astronomy olympiad 1999.
- Company work: e.g. enzymes for monogastric feed with Agroceres Multimix (2014-); whey-protein product for Casa do
  Requeijão via Sebrae (2013).
